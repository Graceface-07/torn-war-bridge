## 🚀 SUPER SIMPLE - 2 Steps:

### Step 1: Run the Script

**Windows:**
```
run-web.bat
```

**Mac/Linux:**
```
./run-web.sh
```

### Step 2: Open Your Browser

Go to: **http://localhost:3000**

**THAT'S IT!** 🎉

---

## 🎯 How to Use:

1. **Enter your Torn User ID** (your player ID)
2. **Enter enemy Faction ID** (the faction you want to scan)
3. **Click "Start Scan"**

The dashboard will:
- ✅ Scan all faction members
- ✅ Show who you can beat (color-coded)
- ✅ Give you war analysis
- ✅ Show priority targets

**All in your web browser!**

---

## 📊 What You'll See:

**Scan Results:**
- 🟢 **Secure** - Easy targets (you're way stronger)
- 🔵 **Prime** - Good targets (you're stronger)
- 🟠 **Risky** - Challenging (similar strength)
- 🔴 **Suicide** - Very hard (they're stronger)

**War Analysis:**
- Win rate percentage
- Tactical verdict
- Top priority targets
- Strategic recommendations

WEB DASHBOARD (most common):
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Run:     run-web.bat  (Windows)
    OR:  ./run-web.sh  (Mac/Linux)

Shows:   http://localhost:3000

Use:     Open in your web browser


DISCORD BOT (for team use):
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Run:     setup-bot.bat  (Windows)
    OR:  ./setup-bot.sh  (Mac/Linux)

Shows:   https://discord.com/api/oauth2/authorize?client_id=...

Use:     Click to invite bot to Discord server
